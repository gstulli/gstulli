## ⭐ Featured project: Universal Safe ID (USI)

**A universal personal identity that is unique, private and recoverable, built as a public good on open source code and a verifiable public registry.**

USI proposes ownerless, non-profit infrastructure that lets anyone prove they are a unique human being, without revealing who they are, while keeping their personal data under their own control. No token, no fees for the citizen, no personal data on the public registry.

The project is in its **conception phase**, with the **whitepaper v0.1.5** published as an open draft. Nothing is final: the proposals are hypotheses to be tested.

**I'm looking for critical readers and collaborators**, especially people with experience in:

cryptography and security protocols · digital identity and W3C standards · distributed systems · digital law and data protection (LGPD/GDPR) · UX for diverse audiences · community building and fundraising

Well-founded criticism is as valuable as new ideas.
